02

GEX — dealer hedging bends the chartGEX — dealer hedging chart ਮੋੜਦਾ

The options market is 10–20× the notional of equities. The tail wags the dog. When you buy a call, the dealer sells it and must buy shares to hedge; as price rises they buy more, as it falls they sell. Mechanical, direction-blind, and it amplifies or dampens every move.Options ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ equities ਤੋਂ 10–20× ਵੱਡਾ। ਜਦੋਂ ਤੁਸੀਂ call ਖਰੀਦੋ, dealer hedge ਲਈ shares ਖਰੀਦਦਾ; ਵਧਣ ਤੇ ਹੋਰ, ਡਿੱਗਣ ਤੇ ਵੇਚਦਾ। Mechanical।

Positive GEX · stabiliserPositive GEX · ਸਥਿਰ

Calls dominate. Dealers buy dips, sell rips. Price mean-reverts toward big call strikes. Low-VIX regime.Calls ਜ਼ਿਆਦਾ। Dealers dips ਖਰੀਦਦੇ, rips ਵੇਚਦੇ। Mean-reversion।

Negative GEX · amplifierNegative GEX · ਵਧਾਉਣ ਵਾਲਾ

Puts dominate. Dealers sell as it falls, buy as it rises. Violent trending conditions. 2020 and 2022 both deeply negative.Puts ਜ਼ਿਆਦਾ। Dealers ਡਿੱਗਣ ਤੇ ਵੇਚਦੇ। ਤਿੱਖੇ trends। 2020, 2022।

03

Order-flow toxicity — the early warningOrder-flow toxicity — ਅਗਾਊਂ ਚੇਤਾਵਨੀ

When a market maker fills your order it hopes you are random. If you are a fund with information, it just bought something about to drop — that is toxic flow. VPIN (López de Prado, Easley, O'Hara) measures the buy/sell imbalance in volume buckets: |buy − sell| ÷ total, from 0 to 1. It spiked for HOURS before the 2010 Flash Crash.MM ਤੁਹਾਡਾ order ਭਰਦਾ ਤੇ ਉਮੀਦ ਕਰਦਾ ਤੁਸੀਂ random ਹੋ। ਜੇ ਜਾਣਕਾਰੀ ਵਾਲਾ fund ਹੋ — toxic flow। VPIN volume buckets ਵਿੱਚ |buy − sell| ÷ total ਮਾਪਦਾ, 0 ਤੋਂ 1। 2010 Flash Crash ਤੋਂ ਘੰਟੇ ਪਹਿਲਾਂ ਵਧਿਆ।

The full stackਪੂਰਾ stack

Every tick is the output of interacting systems: microwave physics, LP routing, latency hedging, dealer gamma, off-exchange flow, toxicity, stop hunts. None are on your chart. All of them shape it.ਹਰ tick ਕਈ ਆਪਸ ਵਿੱਚ ਜੁੜੇ systems ਦਾ ਨਤੀਜਾ। ਕੋਈ ਵੀ chart ਉੱਤੇ ਨਹੀਂ। ਸਾਰੇ ਉਸਨੂੰ ਬਣਾਉਂਦੇ।
