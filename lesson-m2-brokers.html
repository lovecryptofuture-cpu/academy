01

Liquidity: trading without moving the marketLiquidity: ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਹਿਲਾਏ ਬਿਨਾਂ trade

High liquidityਉੱਚੀ liquidity

Many buyers and sellers. Tight spreads, fast fills, minimal slippage. EUR/USD, BTC, AAPL, SPY.ਬਹੁਤ ਖਰੀਦਦਾਰ ਤੇ ਵਿਕਰੇਤਾ। ਤੰਗ spreads, ਤੇਜ਼ fills।

Low liquidityਘੱਟ liquidity

Few participants, wide spreads, partial fills, violent moves. Exotic forex, micro-caps, penny stocks.ਘੱਟ ਭਾਗੀਦਾਰ, ਚੌੜੇ spreads, ਅਧੂਰੀਆਂ fills।

Think rare car vs Honda Civic: one sells in minutes, the other may take months at any price. Liquidity is what makes execution possible on demand.ਦੁਰਲੱਭ ਕਾਰ ਬਨਾਮ Honda Civic: ਇੱਕ ਮਿੰਟਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ ਵਿਕਦੀ, ਦੂਜੀ ਮਹੀਨੇ ਲੈਂਦੀ।

03

A-book vs B-book — if you lose, who wins?A-book ਬਨਾਮ B-book — ਤੁਸੀਂ ਹਾਰੋ ਤਾਂ ਕੌਣ ਜਿੱਤਦਾ?

74–89% of retail CFD accounts lose money (ESMA disclosures). If ~8 in 10 lose and the broker is the counterparty, losing clients are the revenue. That business model has a name.74–89% retail CFD accounts ਹਾਰਦੇ ਹਨ। ਜੇ broker counterparty ਹੈ, ਹਾਰਨ ਵਾਲੇ ਗਾਹਕ ਆਮਦਨ ਹਨ।

A-book (STP / ECN)A-book (STP / ECN)

Broker passes your trade to the real market. Earns spread + commission. Does NOT want you to lose. Interests aligned.Broker trade ਅਸਲੀ ਬਾਜ਼ਾਰ ਨੂੰ ਭੇਜਦਾ। Spread + commission। ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਹਾਰਨਾ ਨਹੀਂ ਚਾਹੁੰਦਾ।

B-book (market maker)B-book (market maker)

Broker IS your counterparty. Your loss = their profit. No real market order placed. Most "zero commission" brokers.Broker ਤੁਹਾਡਾ counterparty। ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਨੁਕਸਾਨ = ਉਨ੍ਹਾਂ ਦਾ ਮੁਨਾਫ਼ਾ।

B-book is not automatically a scam. Internalising is legal and often efficient — small uncorrelated flow nets against itself. Most brokers run a HYBRID: profitable, large or scalping accounts get routed to A-book; the rest stay in-house. If your strategy is genuinely profitable you are probably being A-booked — and your fills are real.B-book ਆਪਣੇ ਆਪ scam ਨਹੀਂ। ਜ਼ਿਆਦਾਤਰ brokers HYBRID ਚਲਾਉਂਦੇ: ਮੁਨਾਫ਼ੇ ਵਾਲੇ ਖਾਤੇ A-book, ਬਾਕੀ ਅੰਦਰ। ਜੇ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਸੱਚਮੁੱਚ profitable ਹੋ, ਤੁਸੀਂ A-booked ਹੋ।
